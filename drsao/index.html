Meet Your Doctor

A Trusted Name in Hair & Skin Care

Dr. Prajct Sao is a well-known Consultant Dermatologist, Trichologist and Hair Transplant Surgeon based in central Mumbai. He completed his MBBS from the prestigious Seth G. S. Medical College & KEM Hospital and his MD in Dermatology from Grant Medical College & Sir J. J. Hospital, Mumbai.

With more than 15 years of experience, he has built a strong reputation for advanced FUE hair transplant surgery, having performed thousands of successful procedures. He is equally respected for his careful, root-cause approach to skin disorders — from acne and pigmentation to psoriasis and paediatric skin conditions.

Patients value Dr. Sao for taking the time to explain their condition clearly, setting realistic expectations, and recommending only the treatment that is genuinely needed.
