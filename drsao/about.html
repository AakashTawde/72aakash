Professional Profile

Dedicated to Hair & Skin Wellness

Dr. Prajct Sao is a respected Consultant Dermatologist, Trichologist and Hair Transplant Surgeon practising in central Mumbai. Over a career spanning more than 15 years, he has helped thousands of patients address hair loss, skin disorders and cosmetic concerns with a calm, methodical and patient-first approach.

He is the director of Cozmaa — Aesthetic, Hair Transplant & Skin Clinic, where he leads a team focused on safe, modern and result-oriented dermatology. He is particularly well known for advanced Follicular Unit Extraction (FUE) hair transplant surgery, having performed several thousand successful procedures.

Beyond surgery, Dr. Sao has deep expertise in medical dermatology — treating acne, pigmentation, psoriasis, fungal infections and paediatric skin conditions — as well as aesthetic treatments such as laser hair reduction, anti-ageing therapy and scar revision.
