Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is FUE hair transplant painful?

The procedure is done under local anaesthesia — you stay awake but feel no pain. Some mild soreness for 24–48 hours is normal.

How long do hair transplant results last?

FUE results are permanent — the transplanted follicles are taken from the resistant zone at the back of the scalp.

Do you do same-day skin consultations?

Yes, depending on availability. WhatsApp the clinic and we will book the earliest slot.

How many PRP sessions are needed?

Most patients see good response in 4–6 sessions, spaced 3–4 weeks apart, followed by maintenance.

Is laser hair reduction safe for Indian skin?

Yes — we use diode and Nd:YAG lasers that are safe for darker skin tones when delivered correctly.
