FUE Hair Transplant: what to expect

Everything you should know before considering a hair transplant.

Hair Transplant  ·  6 min read  ·  Reviewed by Dr. Prajct Sao

Hair loss can affect confidence at any age. For many people who have tried medicines and therapies without lasting results, a hair transplant offers a permanent solution. The most widely used technique today is Follicular Unit Extraction (FUE). This article explains what FUE is, who it suits and what the journey looks like.

What is FUE hair transplant?

FUE is a minimally invasive procedure in which individual hair follicles are carefully removed from a "donor" area — usually the back and sides of the scalp, where hair is genetically resistant to balding — and transplanted into thinning or bald areas. Because follicles are extracted one at a time, FUE does not leave a long linear scar.

Who is a good candidate?

FUE may be suitable if you:

  • Have stable, established pattern hair loss
  • Have a healthy donor area with enough hair density
  • Are in good general health
  • Have realistic expectations about the outcome

A consultation is essential. The doctor assesses your scalp, hair pattern, donor density and overall health before confirming whether you are a candidate.

Tip: A hair transplant restores hair where it has been lost, but it does not stop future thinning of your existing native hair. A good plan often combines surgery with ongoing medical care.

The procedure, step by step

1. Consultation and planning

The doctor designs a natural hairline, estimates the number of grafts needed and explains the expected result. This is the most important stage.

2. Preparation

The donor and recipient areas are cleaned and numbed with local anaesthesia, so the procedure is comfortable and you stay awake throughout.

3. Graft extraction

Individual follicular units are extracted from the donor area using a precise micro-tool.

4. Implantation

Tiny sites are created in the recipient area and the grafts are placed at the correct angle and direction to look completely natural.

Recovery and results

FUE is usually a day-care procedure. Most people return to routine activities within a few days, following simple aftercare instructions. It is normal for the transplanted hair to shed in the first few weeks — this is part of the natural cycle. New growth typically begins after a few months, with fuller results visible around 9 to 12 months.

Is it permanent?

Because transplanted follicles are taken from balding-resistant areas, the results are long-lasting. However, individual results vary depending on age, hair characteristics and how well aftercare is followed.

Important: This article is for general awareness. The right approach for you can only be decided after a personal examination. Always consult a qualified dermatologist or hair transplant surgeon.
