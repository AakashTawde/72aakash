Professional Profile

Dedicated to Hair & Skin Wellness

Dr. Prajct Sao is a respected Consultant Dermatologist, Trichologist and Hair Transplant Surgeon practising in central Mumbai. Over 15+ years he has helped thousands of patients with hair restoration and the everyday skin concerns that quietly matter most.

He directs Cozmaa — an Aesthetic, Hair Transplant & Skin Clinic that pairs modern medicine with the time to actually listen. He is particularly recognised for advanced Follicular Unit Extraction (FUE) hair transplant surgery.
