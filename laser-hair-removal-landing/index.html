The science

Selective photothermolysis,
tuned to you.

Our 808nm diode targets melanin in the follicle, heating it to the point of permanent disablement — without damaging the surrounding skin. We adjust fluence, pulse and cooling to your tone and sensitivity, every single session.

02

03

Questions

Things people ask quietly.

Does it actually hurt?
Most clients rate it 1–3 out of 10 — closer to a warm flick than a wax. Numbing cream is included on sensitive areas like the bikini line.
Is it safe for darker skin?
Yes. Our 808nm diode is gold-standard for Fitzpatrick IV–VI. We patch test at consultation and adjust fluence to your tone.
How many sessions will I need?
Most clients see 80–90% reduction after 6 sessions, spaced 4–6 weeks apart. Hormonal areas (face, lower back) sometimes need 8–10.
Are results permanent?
Yes — laser causes permanent reduction. Hormonal changes (pregnancy, PCOS, menopause) can stimulate dormant follicles, so we offer annual touch-ups.
What should I avoid before a session?
Skip waxing or plucking for 4 weeks, sun and self-tan for 2 weeks. Shave the area 24 hours before. That's it.
