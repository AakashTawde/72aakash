Hair Restoration

Hair Loss & Hair Transplant

Solutions for hair fall, thinning and baldness — from medical therapy to surgical restoration.

FUE Hair Transplant

Follicular Unit Extraction transplants healthy hair follicles to thinning or bald areas for natural, permanent results.

  • Minimally invasive, no linear scar
  • Natural hairline design
  • Day-care procedure

PRP Hair Therapy

Platelet-Rich Plasma uses your own blood platelets to strengthen weak follicles and encourage new growth.

  • Non-surgical, quick sessions
  • Reduces active hair fall
  • Supports transplant results

Hair Fall & Trichology

Detailed scalp analysis to find the real cause of hair fall, followed by a targeted medical treatment plan.

  • Scalp & hair root assessment
  • Treatment for dandruff & scalp issues
  • Nutrition & lifestyle guidance

Hair Loss Medical Management

Evidence-based medication and therapy for male and female pattern hair loss and alopecia.

  • Customised treatment plans
  • Safe, monitored medication
  • Long-term hair maintenance

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Is a hair transplant permanent?

Transplanted follicles are taken from areas resistant to balding, so the results are long-lasting. Dr. Sao will explain what results are realistic for your specific hair pattern.

How do I know which treatment I need?

That is exactly what the consultation is for. After examining you, Dr. Sao recommends only the treatment that is genuinely suited to your condition.

Are the treatments safe?

All treatments are performed using established, evidence-based techniques and modern equipment. Safety and patient comfort are the top priority.

How soon will I see results?

It depends on the treatment and the condition. Results for skin treatments and hair therapy build gradually; your expected timeline is explained during consultation.
