Meet Your Doctor

A trusted name in hair & skin care

Dr. Prajct Sao is a Consultant Dermatologist, Trichologist and Hair Transplant Surgeon based in central Mumbai. He completed his MBBS from Seth G. S. Medical College & KEM Hospital and his MD in Dermatology from Grant Medical College & Sir J. J. Hospital.

Over 15+ years he has helped thousands of patients with advanced FUE hair transplant surgery and careful, root-cause treatment of skin concerns — from acne and pigmentation to psoriasis and paediatric skin conditions.
